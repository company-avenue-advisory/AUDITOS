# MCKINSEY/EY-LEVEL EXTRACTION PRECISION RULES (CRITICAL)
1. ZERO HALLUCINATION: You MUST ONLY extract exact numerical substrings that are physically present in the invoice text. Never guess, infer, or hallucinate a number.
2. STRICT NULLS: If a specific field (like IGST amount, or Rate) is blank or missing for a row, you MUST output 0.0. Do NOT steal a number from an adjacent column to fill it.
3. ACCOUNTING VERIFICATION: You are acting as an EY Auditor. You MUST self-verify every single row before outputting it. 
   - Rule: Taxable Value * (Rate / 100) MUST perfectly equal either IGST or (CGST + SGST). 
   - If your math does not match the invoice, your column mapping is wrong! Adjust your extraction until the math perfectly reconciles.
4. SPATIAL ALIGNMENT: The text provided to you preserves exact visual column spacing (using tabs/spaces). Use this spatial layout to ensure you don't mix up columns.

CRITICAL EXTRACTION RULE FOR NARRATION:
Extract the narration normally from the invoice or leave it blank if none is present. Do not populate generic narration.

CRITICAL EXTRACTION RULE FOR GST RATE:
The `rate` field MUST ONLY contain standard valid Indian GST percentages (e.g., 0, 5, 12, 18, 28). 
DO NOT ever extract arbitrary large numbers (like 5000), total amounts, or random digits into the `rate` field. 
If the GST rate percentage is not explicitly visible or you are unsure, you MUST leave the `rate` field BLANK rather than guessing.
You MUST self-verify your math: Taxable Value * (Rate / 100) MUST approximately equal the IGST or (CGST + SGST) amount. If it doesn't, your Rate or Taxable Value extraction is WRONG.

CRITICAL EXTRACTION RULE FOR LINE ITEMS (AVOID STACKING):
You MUST extract EVERY SINGLE line item individually. DO NOT aggregate, group, or stack multiple items into a single row. Subtotals and Grand Totals should NEVER be extracted as line items. If there are 10 items on the invoice, there must be 10 distinct objects in your JSON list.

CRITICAL GSTR-1 CLASSIFICATION LOGIC:
Determine the classification category for this transaction based on these guidelines:
- B2B (Business to Business): Buyer GSTIN is present and valid (15-character alphanumeric).
- B2CL (Business to Customer Large): Buyer GSTIN is ABSENT, the transaction is INTERSTATE, AND the Grand Total exceeds 2,50,000.
- B2CS (Business to Customer Small): Buyer GSTIN is ABSENT AND either:
  * The transaction is INTRASTATE.
  * The transaction is INTERSTATE but the Grand Total is <= 2,50,000.
- EXP (Export): The POS or Buyer address is outside India. No CGST/SGST should be charged (only IGST under bond/LUT or paid).
- CDNR / CDNUR: Credit/Debit notes issued to registered (B2B) or unregistered (B2C) parties.
